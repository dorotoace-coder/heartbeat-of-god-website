HBG PULSE OS — APP TUTORIAL SOURCE PACK

Guide edition 03 · 28 November 2026

Purpose: teach current app navigation, one task per short clip. This is product help, not ministry curriculum.

Production brief: calm minimal design; white and deep purple, clear captions, slow pointer movements. Use fictional demonstration records only. Do not show credentials, real names, contact details, private requests or financial records.

Create a separate 35–75 second clip for each scripted role below. Keep actual button labels. Do not invent menus or imply broader permissions. Use the sign-in overview as the common introduction. End every clip by checking the save confirmation.

Reading a guide does not grant access. Real work stays inside each person’s assigned role.

Do not present scripts as finished videos. No videos have been exported in this pack.

START HERE

1. Sign in to HBG PulseOS with your own email and password. Use Create an account if you are new.

2. Choose Sign in. Your member home or assigned staff workspace opens. A new account does not automatically receive department access.

3. Open Pulse OS Help whenever you need a reminder. Read any guide; work only in the sections your role permits.

4. Before saving, check the person, service, class or file. Wait for the success message. Sign out when using a shared device.



## Preparing to serve on Sunday

Audience: Workers and assigned platform team

Start: Pulse OS Help

Confirm your duty, prepare for worship and follow the service cues.

1. Confirm your assignment, arrival time and supervisor before Sunday.

2. Wear clean, modest and practical clothing suited to your duty. Ask the lead about any uniform or platform dress guidance.

3. If assigned a platform duty, prepare your contribution, check pronunciations and agree your time and closing cue.

4. Ask permission before sharing a testimony and take sensitive matters to the responsible lead in private.

5. After service, hand over any open tasks and agree follow-up with your supervisor.

Check: Your lead confirms your duty and any rehearsal or follow-up.

If something goes wrong: If you expect to arrive late or cannot serve, contact the coordinator so the backup can prepare.



## Worker service check-in

Audience: Pastor records arrivals; workers confirm details with their leader

Start: Training → Worker service check-in

Record an enrolled worker’s arrival for a scheduled service in Lagos time.

1. Refresh Pulse OS, open Training and expand Worker service check-in.

2. Choose the service and enrolled worker. If the worker list is empty, use Training administration to enrol the worker first.

3. Enter the actual arrival time in Lagos time and an optional explanation or correction note.

4. Choose Save worker check-in and wait for Check-in saved. Saving the same worker and service updates that entry.

Check: Look for Check-in saved. Arrival times are recorded in Lagos time.

If something goes wrong: If saving cannot be confirmed, retain the entries and retry the same worker and service. Ask the Pastor about a missing service or worker.



## Mentors and supervised service

Audience: Pastor, assigned mentors, supervisors and enrolled learners

Start: Training → Mentoring and supervised service

Connect learning with a named mentor, practical assignment and pastoral review.

1. The Pastor chooses the learner, formation stage, mentor and service supervisor.

2. Enter a practical assignment and review date, then choose Assign supervised service.

3. The assigned mentor or supervisor opens the placement and records observed service and feedback. Write feedback suitable for the learner to read.

4. The Pastor records a review outcome and agreed next step. Review the learner’s calling, character and practice when considering responsibility.

Check: The saved placement, observations and reviews appear in the mentoring section.

If something goes wrong: If no learners appear, enrol them through Training administration. If records are unavailable, refresh and ask the Pastor to check the assignment.



## Foundation graduation and department intake

Audience: Foundation teachers, Pastor, graduates and department leads

Start: Training → Foundation School; Foundation School register

Complete learning and review, then arrange graduation and supervised department service.

1. Follow the four Sunday lessons and record actual participation in the Foundation School register. Arrange recovery teaching for missed sessions.

2. Complete the knowledge check and the authorised attendance and practical review.

3. Agree the graduate list and ceremony date with the Pastor. The proposed ceremony includes Scripture, certificates, pastoral prayer and introductions to department leads.

4. Discuss gifts, availability and a preferred department. The Pastor and receiving lead agree a supervised assignment, induction and review date.

5. Use Mentoring and supervised service to record the placement. The proposed first review follows four services. Complete role-specific approvals before duties begin.

Check: Check lesson and review records and the receiving department’s agreed placement. The ceremony and four-service review are proposals for pastoral confirmation.

If something goes wrong: Arrange catch-up for unfinished learning. Ask the receiving lead to confirm responsibilities before starting an assignment.



## Children’s Church teacher training

Audience: Children’s Church teachers and supervising leaders

Start: Training → Children’s Church; Departments → Children’s Church

Prepare one shared Bible lesson with activities suited to different ages.

1. Open Children’s Church in Training. The revised course is a draft for pastoral review.

2. Complete the shared teacher induction and rehearse a short lesson with the lead teacher. Follow the ministry’s approved child-protection and caregiver handover arrangements.

3. Prepare one story, one truth, one short verse and one response. Use pictures and movement for ages 2–5, retelling for ages 6–8, and reading and discussion for ages 9–12.

4. Use the eight-Sunday starter sequence in the lesson. Share one optional home conversation with caregivers.

5. After the session, review what helped children learn and agree one improvement. Record supervised teacher feedback through the assigned placement.

Check: The supervising leader confirms readiness and the next teaching assignment. Check the module’s approval status before using the revised teaching.

If something goes wrong: Ask the lead teacher for support with age adaptations or staffing. Children under two need their caregiver or an approved separately staffed arrangement.

## Treasury

Audience: Pastor and Treasury Leader

Start: Treasury → Record a collection

Record a collection, obtain independent confirmation and track a bank deposit.

1. Choose the correct service before entering the collection.

2. Choose Giving category, Payment method and Currency, then enter Amount. Select a giver when that category requires one.

3. For cash, enter two distinct cash counters. For other payment methods, complete Verified by.

4. Choose Record collection. The other authorized reviewer uses Awaiting independent confirmation → Confirm collection after checking it.

5. For a bank deposit, open Cash sent to the bank. Choose the service, currency, cash deposited and bank reference, then Record bank deposit. The other reviewer verifies it against the bank record.

6. Read Recent monthly totals carefully: pending amounts and confirmed amounts are separate.

Check: A saved collection can still be pending. Look for the saved entry and its independent confirmation; do not treat pending money as reconciled.

If something goes wrong: If the save is unverified, keep the page open and retry the same collection. Check the saved list before creating another entry. Ask the authorized reviewer about corrections.

Target length: 75 seconds

Narration:

Open Treasury and choose the correct service. Select the category, payment method and currency, then enter the amount. Choose a giver when required. Cash needs two distinct counters. Select Record collection and wait for confirmation. The other authorized reviewer checks the entry and confirms it. Pending and confirmed totals are different. Record a bank deposit separately and have the other reviewer verify it against the bank record. If a save is unverified, retry the same details instead of creating another collection.

Storyboard: 0–5s title and task; 5–15s open workspace; middle show the listed steps; final 10s show the saved result and one recovery tip.

If something goes wrong: If an upload pauses, select the same file to retry. Check the file list first. Ask your leader if the file or shared folder cannot be accessed.

For a video over fifty megabytes, use the shared Google Drive folder and add its link under Large videos. Open Caption and review. Save the caption draft, add a title and intended use, and submit for Pastor review. A saved upload is private; approval does not automatically publish it. If an upload pauses, retry the same file and check the list before starting again.

The mark saves when you press the button, so check the saved message before continuing. Unmarked is not the same as absent. If your class or a student is missing, ask the Pastor to check the class assignment or enrolment.

## Visitor & ministry follow-up

Audience: Assigned outreach workers and Pastor

Start: Follow-up → Add a follow-up

Keep the next conversation, responsible worker and outcome clear.

1. For a new request, enter Full name, choose the Reason and Next contact date, and confirm contact permission.

2. The Pastor can choose a worker or leave the request Unassigned. Workers create requests assigned to themselves.

3. Choose Add follow-up. Find it under Overdue, Due today or Upcoming; use Find a person, Source or Assignment to narrow the list.

4. Open Record a contact or handoff. Choose What happened?, write a short factual note and set the next date when needed.

5. For a handoff, choose another registered worker. For completion or no further contact, choose the matching outcome. Select Save follow-up.

6. Use View follow-up history to read the saved outcome. Closed requests remain closed.

Check: Check the updated lane, next date or closed label, then read the saved history. After handoff, the previous worker loses access.

If something goes wrong: If saving is unverified, retry the same details or refresh to inspect the record. Do not create a second request just because a response was slow.

Target length: 65 seconds

Narration:

Open Follow-up. Add the name, reason and next contact date, then confirm permission. The Pastor may assign a worker or leave the request unassigned. Save it and find it in the board. Open Record a contact or handoff, choose the outcome and add a brief factual note. Set the next date for an open request. For a handoff, select the next registered worker. Save and check the history. Completed requests and requests for no further contact stay closed.

Storyboard: 0–5s title and task; 5–15s open workspace; middle show the listed steps; final 10s show the saved result and one recovery tip.



## Past program contacts

Audience: Pastor (owner account)

Start: Follow-up → Past program contacts

Find past ILPC and Mighty Turn Around registrants, see what Pulse OS knows about each one, and prepare a follow-up linked to them.

1. Open Follow-up. Past program contacts is above Add a follow-up. Use Find a contact, Program, Ministry relationship and Attendance to narrow the list.

2. Read each card: Worker account matched (with the department), Member confirmed, Membership unconfirmed or Needs review (with the reason), and the attendance. Unknown means not yet recorded, not absent.

3. The list refreshes every minute and keeps showing the last results if a refresh fails. The message says whether to check your access or your connection.

4. To follow someone up, choose Prepare follow-up. The form says it is linked and keeps the name fixed. Choose the reason, next contact date and worker, and confirm the person agreed to follow-up. Choose Add follow-up.

5. Choose Remove link first if you want a separate follow-up that is not linked to this contact; the permission box clears so you confirm it again.

6. Contacts who need review, already have an open follow-up or asked for no further contact have no Prepare follow-up button.

Check: Follow-up created appears, and the contact's card shows Follow-up open with the next contact date. Closed follow-ups stay in the card's history.

If something goes wrong: If a save is not confirmed, choose Add follow-up again with the same details; it cannot create a second follow-up. If the save is refused, the message explains why and the list refreshes. Appearing in this list is not permission to send newsletters or invitations.



## Linking contacts to people

Audience: Pastor (owner account)

Start: Past program contacts → Link to a person; People

Connect a program contact, their registrations and a member profile to one person, with the evidence recorded and every step reversible.

1. On a contact card, choose Link to a person. Pulse OS suggests people with the same email, phone or name and shows why. It never chooses for you.

2. Choose a suggested person or New person. Tick only the evidence you actually have, add a note if helpful, and tick I checked this is the same person. Choose Save link.

3. A linked card shows Linked to and the person's name. Choose Show registrations to see Mighty Turn Around registrations with the same email. Link each one to the person only after checking it.

4. In People, open the person to see their linked records and follow-ups, and their earlier details. Restore these details puts earlier details back; the details you replace are kept too.

5. Reviewing a member profile in People also links it to the chosen person, and the person's earlier details are kept.

6. If a link is wrong, choose Unlink and give a short reason. The person and the registration stay; the link is kept in history as removed. An unlinked member profile returns to the review queue.

Check: The card shows Linked to and the person's name, and People shows the person with their linked records. People created from contacts show From program contacts · membership unconfirmed until a reviewed member profile confirms membership, and are not offered for Foundation School unless they asked for it.

If something goes wrong: If a save is not confirmed, choose Save link again unchanged. If the contact was linked meanwhile, the list refreshes. Never link by name alone. Linking does not give permission to contact someone, membership or staff access.



## Pastorate

Audience: Lead Pastor approves; assigned pastors read their own assignment

Start: Pastorate

Give a pastor a written assignment with clear limits and a review date, and use the Sunday rehearsal steps.

1. Open Pastorate. The Lead Pastor sees Pastoral access and assignments; an assigned pastor sees My pastoral assignments.

2. Lead Pastor: enter the Pastor’s name, their Existing login email, the Responsibility, the Assignment and authority limits, and a Review date. Choose Approve pastoral assignment.

3. Saving the same login again replaces that pastor’s assignment. The pastor must already have a Pulse OS login; this does not create one.

4. To end an assignment, choose Revoke Pastorate access on it and wait for Pastorate access revoked.

5. Before Sunday, work through Sunday rehearsal with the team. Record a trainee’s supervised service through Open training and placements.

Check: Assignment saved appears and the assignment is listed as Active with its review date. The assigned pastor sees it under My pastoral assignments with their own login.

If something goes wrong: If Assignment could not be saved appears, check the login email belongs to an existing account, then your access and connection. A Pastorate assignment does not enrol training, open Treasury or grant other department access.

3. Enter first-timers as a count already included within that total; do not add them again.
