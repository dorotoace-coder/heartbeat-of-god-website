Heartbeat of God Ministry — Sunday Service Announcement
To be read from the pulpit by Pastor Amos Unogwu
Sunday, June 7, 2026
MTA 2026 —
The Mighty Turn Around Assembly
[ Stand to your feet if you can. ] Church, I want to talk to you for just a few minutes. We have just concluded ILPC 2026 — three days of fresh oil, fresh fire, and fresh encounters with God. And I want to say to every one of you — thank you. Thank you for showing up. Thank you for believing. God was here.
But church — we are not stopping here. In fact, what God just poured into you at ILPC is not the destination. It is the fuel. Because what is coming next is even greater. I want to announce to you — MTA 2026. "There is a river whose streams make glad the city of God, the holy place where the Most High dwells." — Psalm 46 : 4 The Mighty Turn Around Assembly. This September — September 4th, 5th, and 6th — we are gathering again. And this time, we are coming for the turnaround. Not just a blessing. Not just a word. A turnaround — in your health, your family, your finances, your ministry, your destiny.
But before MTA, I want you to know — beginning August 13th, this entire ministry enters into 21 days of corporate fasting and prayer. We are calling it — We Wait. We Wait — 21 days. August 13 to September 2. You will fast with us. You will pray with us. You will seek God with us for 21 days — and then on September 4th, we walk straight out of the fast and into the assembly. You don't come to MTA empty. You come loaded — loaded from 21 days in the presence of God.
[ Lower your voice — then rise with fire: ] Church, I am telling you — something is going to break at MTA 2026. Things that have been stuck for years are going to move. Situations that have refused to shift are going to turn around. Because there is a river — and it is about to flood this place. MTA 2026 — September 4, 5 and 6! It is free. It is open to all. We will be in person here at HBG Ministry, Akute and online for those who cannot make it physically. [ Hold up your phone / point to the screen: ] Go to — mta.heartbeatofgod.ca — register yourself, register your family, register your neighbour, register your colleague. Registration is completely free. Tell somebody. Send a message. Make a call today.
Can somebody shout — "My turnaround is coming!" [ Congregation responds — then dismiss / continue service. ]
