Getting to the Venue

Step-by-step directions from anywhere in Lagos — easy, local, and stress-free.

📍   100/102 Akute Road, opp. Royal Prince Supermarket, Akute

Choose your starting point ↓

🚌

From Ojodu-Berger

Most popular route · Fastest connection

~30 min
1

Get to Ojodu-Berger Bus Stop

From anywhere on Lagos Mainland, board a bus heading to Ojodu-Berger. This is your main interchange point.

🚐 Danfo 🚌 BRT/ABC 🚗 Uber/Bolt
2

Board a bus going to "Akute" or "Lambe"

At Berger, find a danfo or minibus going to Akute. Tell the conductor "Akute junction" or "Royal Prince Supermarket".

🚐 Danfo to Akute 🛺 Keke NAPEP
💡 Fare is usually ₦200–₦400 from Berger
3

Drop at Royal Prince Supermarket

Tell the driver to drop you at Royal Prince Supermarket on Akute Road. The venue is right there — 100/102 Akute Road.

🏁 You have arrived!
🌉

From Lagos Island / CMS

Via Third Mainland Bridge → Berger

~60–90 min
1

Board a bus from CMS or TBS

At CMS Bus Stop or Tafawa Balewa Square, take any bus heading to Ikorodu Road → Ketu → Ojodu-Berger.

🚌 BRT Lagos 🚐 Danfo
2

Drop at Ojodu-Berger

This is your transfer point. From here follow Route 1 steps 2 & 3 above to get to Akute.

🔁 Follow Route 1 from here
✈️

From Ikeja / Airport

Via Agege Motor Road or Pen Cinema

~45 min
1

Head to Pen Cinema or Agege

From Ikeja or the airport, take a bus or keke toward Pen Cinema junction or Agege Motor Road.

🛺 Keke NAPEP 🚗 Uber/Bolt
2

Connect to Ojodu-Berger

From Pen Cinema or Agege, board any bus to Ojodu-Berger. It's a short ride on the Lagos-Abeokuta Expressway.

🚐 Danfo 🚌 Minibus
3

Berger → Akute → You're there

At Berger, take a danfo or keke to Akute. Drop at Royal Prince Supermarket — venue is right there.

🏁 Total journey under 45 mins
